Guitar, cable, amp.

That is the reference rig. Every other rig on this site is measured against it, which is to say that every other rig is a decision to depart from it.

## The ritual

Plug in. Set every knob to noon. Play for ten minutes. Write down the three things you wish were different.

Then, and only then, ask whether any of those three things is a pedal.

## What this tells you

Usually it is a different amp setting. Sometimes it is how you pick. Occasionally, and we want to be fair about this, it is a pedal. The Boss DS-1, for example, is a perfectly honest answer to "I want it more distorted." You are allowed to have an answer.

## Why no one will keep it

Because a rig this simple has no surprises left for you to be disappointed by. Complexity is a way of staying in the story. The Temple understands. We are here to hold the line, not to enforce it.
