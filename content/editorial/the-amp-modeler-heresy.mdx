There is a faction in the Temple that believes a glowing tube amp, turned up loud in a room, is the only honest source of toan. There is another faction that owns a small box with a screen and has not touched their amp in two years.

They will not be reconciled. This sermon is for both.

## The heretical position

A good modeler can sound very close to the amps it imitates, and it does so quietly, consistently, and without needing a repair. It can recall the same sound every night. It will never be too loud for the neighbors, because it does not have to be. For most people, most of the time, that is not a compromise. That is the point.

## The orthodox position

A tube amp is not only a tone. It is a physical object that moves air, and it responds to your playing in ways that many players describe as feeling more alive. Some of that is the speaker, the cabinet, the volume, and the room, and some of it is you leaning into a machine that is leaning back.

A modeler can approximate that. Whether the approximation is enough is a conversation that has been going on for years and will outlast us.

## What a fair test looks like

1. Pick one amp sound you know well.
2. Record yourself playing through the real amp in a decent room.
3. Record the same part through the modeler into the same speakers or monitors.
4. Have someone else play both back without telling you which is which.

You may be surprised in either direction. The Temple has no wish to predict it.

## The sermon

Use the thing that makes you want to play. If that is a hundred-watt stack, God bless your hearing. If that is a box on the floor of an apartment, God bless your landlord.

The Temple is large. There is a pew for everyone, including the people who disagree with this sermon.
