Bass strings come in two main kinds, and the choice between them is one of the cheapest experiments in the whole hobby. A set costs less than most pedals. Run the experiment.

## What is different

A bass string is a core wire with another wire wrapped around it. The difference is in the wrap.

- **Roundwound** strings use a wrap with a circular cross section. The surface is ridged, like a screw thread.
- **Flatwound** strings use a flat ribbon for the wrap and are then smoothed, so the surface feels slick.

That is the entire physical difference. Everything else is a consequence.

## What you will tend to notice

- **Brightness.** Roundwounds are generally brighter, with more high-frequency content and a more pronounced attack. Flatwounds are generally darker and rounder.
- **Feel.** Flats feel smooth under the fingers and make little finger noise. Rounds feel textured and make the squeaks and slides you hear on records that use them.
- **Sustain and ring.** Rounds tend to ring longer and with more harmonics. Flats tend to sound more focused and "thumpy," with a quicker decay.
- **Wear.** Rounds go dead as the wrap collects grime and loses brightness. Flats are often described as lasting longer in tone, though they are not immortal.

These are tendencies, not laws. Brands, gauges, core shapes, and your own hands all move the result.

## How to test it fairly

1. Pick one bass and one amp setting. Do not touch the knobs.
2. Record a short loop with the old strings, played the same way each time.
3. Change one string at a time, or the whole set, and record the same loop.
4. Listen back later, away from the bass, without looking at which file is which.

If you cannot tell them apart in a blind listen, you have saved money. If you can, you have found your sound without buying a single new pedal.

## The doctrine

The Temple has no position on which is correct. We only note that this is a rare corner of gear culture where the cheap option is a genuine, audible, reversible change. Try it before you buy anything with a footswitch.
