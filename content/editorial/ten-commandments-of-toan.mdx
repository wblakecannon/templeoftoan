1. Thou shalt not buy a pedal because of a video.

2. The cable is part of the rig. Thou shalt not blame the pedal for the cable.

3. Honor thy volume knob. It is the oldest effect and it is free.

4. Thou shalt not say "mojo" about a capacitor thou hast not measured.

5. Remember that the room is also an instrument, and that thou art playing it badly.

6. Thou shalt not covet thy neighbor's pedalboard, for he does not use it either.

7. A pedal that is never turned on has no toan. It is merely furniture.

8. Thou shalt change thy strings before thou changest thy amp.

9. Honor the player. The tone is in the fingers, and the fingers are the only part thou canst not order online.

10. Sacrifice thy wallet if thou must. But know the price, and know why.
