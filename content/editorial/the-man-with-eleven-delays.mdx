He is a composite. We say so up front, because he is also, in some way, someone you know.

He owns eleven delay pedals. Each one is, by his account, doing something the others cannot. He can explain what, in detail, for an amount of time you did not agree to.

## His practice

He plays for twenty minutes, most evenings, mostly the same four chords. The rest of the evening is configuration. He has a spreadsheet. It has a tab for his delays.

## His relic

Like many in the congregation, he is drawn to the germanium fuzz, and holds strong views on the Fuzz Face that he has not yet been able to check with an actual measurement.

## What we learned

Nothing about him is a failure. He is happy, he is curious, and he has found a way to spend time he would otherwise spend worrying about other things. The Temple has no quarrel with him. We are merely taking notes.

If you see yourself in this, welcome. If you do not, count your pedals.
