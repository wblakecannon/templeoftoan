The DS-1 is orange, cheap, and everywhere. Boss released it in 1978, and it has been on more pedalboards than anyone will admit.

## The shame

It has become a pedal people feel they need to excuse. It is the entry-level thing. It is what you buy before you know better. The forums will tell you it has been surpassed, and then a pile of boutique pedals will be sold to you as the answer to the DS-1.

## The sermon

The DS-1 does one job and does it consistently. That consistency is the thing the boutique world has quietly stopped selling you, because it is hard to charge four times as much for a pedal that simply works.

We are not telling you to buy one. We are telling you that the next time someone sneers at an orange box, you may ask them to play something through it.
